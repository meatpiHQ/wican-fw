Since 2026-10-01 (sleep_manager v3, `wake_mv`) a **wake-voltage leg**: sleep 12.9 V / wake 13.4 V with a 1 min delay, 12.5 V = sleep entry, then 13.2 V held 40 s (between the two) must NOT wake the device (supply current stays at the nap level, HTTP dead), 13.6 V must (`power_wake` from `sleep_mode` in the restart history); the restore puts 13.1 / 13.2 V and the 5 min delay back. On a firmware without `wake_mv` the leg is skipped with a note. The **wake hold** (`wake_delay_ms`, v4) is timed from the DEVICE console (the bench opens COM254: seconds between `state: wake_pending` and `waking by reboot (voltage recovered)`; the supply current cannot time it, its own detection latency is ~8 s): the default 0.5 s hold must read 0.3 to 2.6 s, the 5 s hold 4.5 to 7.6 s and at least 3 s more.
