Command: `bash /tmp/trial_gap_bench.sh <channel hint: 11 or 0> [ssid] [NetworkManager profile holding its psk]` on rpi001 (the script is `test-reports/logs/mobiledata_20261008/trial_gap_bench.sh`, copied to the Pi's `/tmp`)

**The WiFi trial as the phone on the access point sees it** (2026-10-08, Ali: "when I click test and continue my phone drops off the device AP then reconnects", and "maybe we switch the channel first then connect"). The Pi's `wtest1` (mt76x2u) is the phone on the DUT's access point (profile `wican-bench-ap`, the DUT's AP password, autoconnect off) with `iw event -t` logging what a client receives; the trial targets a second network on channel 11 (the DUT and the bench AP sit on 6); the DUT's own log times the request to the switch on its ms clock. Measured BEFORE: the switch 3.1 to 3.7 s after the request (the station's all-channel scan, during which the access point cannot beacon), announced over 3 beacons (`channel switch started (count=3)`) and FOLLOWED by the client without a disconnect: the switch is not what drops a phone, the silence before it is, and so is the write of the AP config at got-ip (`sync_ap_channel_to_sta`), which restarts the soft AP and deauthenticates its clients (`station leave, reason = 2` 2 to 7 ms after got-ip, seen twice on the P4). AFTER (the scan row's `channel` in the POST and `WIFI_FAST_SCAN` on it; the re-join straight back to the entry the trial left with its channel first; no AP config write while clients are on the AP, caught up when the last one leaves): the switch 0.4 to 0.9 s after the request, the trial connected with an address in 2.1 s, no kick at got-ip, the re-join of a present home network 0.45 s (was 7.2 s), the client following every switch with no beacon loss.

**The WiFi USB adapters as access points (Ali, 2026-10-08: "the wifi usb adapters had some issues, that is why we used the esp32 dev board"):** a NetworkManager hotspot on the mt76x2u or on the Pi's onboard brcmfmac never completed the trial's handshake (reason 2 after 1.5 to 4.3 s; pmf off and rsn/ccmp made no difference) and twice answered the next trial with `not_found`, hint or not. The same trial against the bench_ap P4 renamed onto channel 11 for a minute (`ap set CSA_PROBE_P4 <psk>`, `ap channel 11`; back with `ap set WICAN_TEST_AP <psk>`, `ap channel 6`; the daemon from `bench_ap_net.sh up`, or `setsid nohup python3 ~/wican/tools/testbench/pi/bench_ap_daemon.py`; opening the daemon resets the board once) connects in 2.1 s, and a wrong then a right password against it 8 s apart give `password` (15) then `connected` in 1.1 s: use the P4 as the second network, the adapters only as the phone (a station, where they behaved). Bench artefact to read around: while the P4 carries the other name the DUT's home network is absent, and the reconnect task's attempts (all-channel scans, the pause budget spent after a trial) silence the access point every few seconds: the client dropped on that 10 s after the trial, not on the trial; a fresh device has no configured network and no such attempts. Runs: adapter hotspot with the hint (A, C, E) 0.4 s to the switch, without (B, F) 3.2 s; P4 run 1 (before the guard) the kick at got-ip; P4 run 3 (guarded) no kick, got-ip at 2.1 s, the client's drop 10 s later on the absent-network scans. Nothing saved, the DUT back on WICAN_TEST_AP after each run; the Pi's radios and the probe hotspot (`csa-probe`, autoconnect off) taken down at the end
