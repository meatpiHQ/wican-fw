# An EU truck end to end: WWH-OBD and J1939 on one bus

Command: `.\test.ps1 eutruck`

Needs: PCAN on the DUT's bus + the ECU simulator (USB-NCM 192.168.8.1) + rpi001 (tunnel, broker)

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**An EU truck end to end: WWH-OBD and J1939 on one bus** (`tools/testbench/obd/eu_truck_e2e_bench.py` → `EU TRUCK E2E PASS`, ~2.5 min, 5 DUT restarts, 3 simulator restarts; then the same bench with `--only x5` → `EU TRUCK E2E PARTIAL PASS (legs: x5)`, ~3 min, 5 restarts; `TASK_j1939_wwh.md` phase 7 and its addendum). The vehicle is `actors/pcan_wwh_ecu.py --truck`: two WWH-OBD ECUs (29-bit, 250 kbit/s) and the J1939 truck of `pcan_j1939_truck.py` in ONE PCAN process (the adapter gives a channel to one client) with one VIN; the simulator acknowledges, then is the car. Legs: **e1** a device that knows an OBD-II car, native bus off: the bus guard refuses the car's protocol on a 250 kbit/s bus, the contact walk finds the truck, the detection stores dialect `uds` + `j1939 true` (from a bus sample) with rows of both sets; **e2** the one restart the Quick Setup stages (`can_manager` listen-only at the measured bit rate + `j1939`): values of both sets equal the actor's at the API and on the Pi's broker, the chip asks `22F4xx` physically only, the native bus transmits nothing; **e3** one DTC report with the `19 42` codes and the DM1 code, MIL from both, the legislated clear (`14 FF FF 33`) takes the WWH codes and leaves DM1 heard; **e4** the stored car (500 kbit/s) made the current vehicle while plugged into the truck (the UI's "use this car"): the bus guard looks again and reads the live 250 kbit/s bus, nothing leaves the chip at the car's bit rate (judged at the truck: its adapter prints the first bus error after a quiet second and the moment it cannot send), then the truck is made current again and polled; **e5** moved back to the car as a device is moved (polling and the native bus off while the bus becomes the car's, then plugged in): the car is current again and polled, the truck stays known. Closing checks as the other PC benches. Second stage, leg **x5**: what the TABLES send to the chip on the truck's bus. `dtc_init` with `ATSP6` at a trouble code scan and a row tested from the UI with such an init answer 409 with the guard's sentence; a row's own init and a type's init polled after a boot are not sent (`bus_guard.refused`, the sentence) and first contact still finds the truck; a reset inside a chain (`ATWS`) is followed by the baseline prelude; with Automate off, a row tested as the first chip traffic of a boot starts from the prelude; zero bus errors at the truck in every step, and the truck never has to be started again (a PCAN adapter that goes bus-off stays off). Optional legs with `--only`: `x1` (the detection repeated N times), `x2` (the truck forgotten before every detection), `x3` (boot, first contact, detection in a loop), with `--detections N`: the hunt for the reset of the first run, which they did not bring back in 271 detections; the console tail did (TASK addendum, finding 28).
