# A unit that ran the factory firmware, flashed with v6 without an erase

Command: `python tools/testbench/system/factory_flash_bench.py [--restore] [--backup auto\|<ip>\|none]` (and `--restore-only BACKUP`)

Needs: DUT console + PSU + rpi001 (wtest1 for the restore)

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**A unit that ran the factory firmware, flashed with v6 without an erase** (prints `FACTORY FLASH PASS`, 18 checks, ~6 min with the restore; 2026-10-06, after Ali's fresh unit showed `1 fault`). The factory firmware (legacy v4.5x) keeps ONE 6 MB LittleFS `storage` partition at 0x9EE000, where v6 has its 256 KB `settings` partition with `storage` behind it; `build.ps1 -Flash` does not erase; LittleFS mounts a superblock whatever block count it claims, so until that day the factory filesystem mounted on the settings partition and every access past 256 KB failed (`E esp_littlefs: failed to read addr ...`, 41 x `persist failed`, `FAULT boot_errors: 83 error lines at boot`, no setting saved). The bench takes a settings backup from the bench lease, ERASES the DUT, writes a factory-style image (littlefs-python: 1536 blocks, the legacy JSON files, two used data blocks where v6's `storage` starts) at 0x9EE000, flashes `@flash_args` without a reset and cold-boots with the console from power-on: 0 E lines, the two W lines (`settings_manager: 'settings' holds a LittleFS of 1536 blocks on a 64-block partition (another firmware's layout, the factory image?): formatting`, `filesystem: 'storage' holds no LittleFS superblock (another firmware's data): formatting`), `WICAN HEALTH errors=0`, `WICAN FAULTS active=0`, `faults` = no fault codes, the first-boot erases within the 64 budget, the AP up; a second cold boot: 0 E lines, no probe line, `WICAN FLASH writes=0 erases=0` (the defaults persisted on boot 1), no fault. `--restore` then puts the backup back through the DUT's AP from rpi001 (wtest1, the path of TESTING.md "Reaching the DUT's AP", a rescan and up to four tries) and judges by observed state: the DUT on its bench lease with its own AP password; `--restore-only BACKUP` does only that for a DUT left fresh (the AP name from the console's `wifi -i`). /data is NOT in the backup and is gone after a run. Host needs `pip install littlefs-python`.

**2026-10-06: PASS** three times on the firmware checks (0 E lines, 26 erases, `active=0` each time); the restore lost its POST answer once and failed to associate once before the retries, then `FACTORY FLASH PASS` end to end at 10:16.
