# The connection trial on hardware

Command: `python tools/testbench/wifi/wifi_try_bench.py [--wican auto] [--dut 10.42.1.194]`

Needs: PC + rpi001 + DUT (console)

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**The connection trial on hardware** (prints `WIFI TRY PASS`, 28 checks, ~2.5 min, no restart): the Pi's `wtest1` on the DUT's access point is the phone (`WICAN_AP_PSK` env = the AP password, default `quick-setup-2026`), the bench AP is the home network (its name and PSK from the Pi's `wican-bench` profile). `right`: the bench PSK gives 202 running, then done / connected with the DUT's address on 10.42.1.x, rssi, channel, took_ms under 20 s (3.9 s at -37 dBm on the bench), the console's `trial: 'X' connected, ...` line, the configured station back on the bench AP within 30 s (5.5 s: the trial's end is a cue that skips the AP-client pause and the backoff, before that cue it took 42 to 60 s); `busy`: three plain POSTs in the SAME ssh hop, stamped (a hop alone can take as long as a trial, and a retried curl lands after it): a 409 among the later ones when the phone's link let them through, a FAIL only when two 202s come within 2.5 s (two trials at once); the bench phone (the Pi's `wtest1`) loses the AP around every radio event (the AP's PMF SA query kicks a client that re-associates after the hop, reason 209, and the IDF driver's `wifi:CCMP replay detected` E line about that client's frame is whitelisted here), so the hop is retried for the first 202, polls re-join the phone after two misses, and the poll budget is 50 s; `wrong`: done / password with reason 15 on the bench AP (204 on other routers), the console line, no `failed N attempts in a row` line (the attempt memory never hears of a trial), the station back; `nowhere`: a made-up name gives not_found / 201 in 2.8 s; `bad`: a 5-character password and a missing ssid give 400; `console`: `wifi --try` prints the connected line; `health`: settings unchanged, the AP still up, no restart, 0 E lines. 2026-10-06: fifteen runs on the day; the last three (17:0x to 17:2x) PASS in a row on the final build, the earlier ones found the console stamps being relative to its open, the busy window and the curl retries, the AP-client pause after a trial (firmware: the restore cue), a got-IP landing while a trial lets go (firmware: let go again), and the Pi adapter's drops (bench tolerance). `tools/testbench/wifi/../..//test-reports/logs/wifi_try_20261006/real_page.log`: the same on the DUT's own page in Chrome through the tunnel (`TRY REAL PASS`: a page that rides the station the test drops still lands on Review with the verdict in 14 s, and a wrong password shows the marked field with reason 15 in 18 s; this run found the POST's answer lost with the station, fixed by the 300 ms deferred start and the page's 4 s POST timeout). 2026-10-08: `WIFI TRY PASS` on the build with the trial's channel hint, the straight re-join and the guarded AP config write (the bench's own POSTs carry no `channel`, so its trials still walk every channel: connected in 3.9 s;
