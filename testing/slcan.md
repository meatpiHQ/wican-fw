**slcan over TCP, and the trap first** (prints `SLCAN BRIDGE PASS`, ~3 min, 2 to 3 planned restarts; born from a Discord report of 2026-10-16: "SLCAN works on the CAN monitor, not over python-can; socket_manager says client connected, nothing arrives", reproduced on the bench as the `slcan0` server switched on under Socket servers with no `can <-slcan-> slcan0` connection row). Leg B, bridged (one restart: `br_slcan = can <-slcan-> slcan0` and `br_can = can <-slcan-> ws_can`, any bridge on ws_can parked): `bridge` names both, python-can over the tunnel receives exactly N (200) frames in order while the /ws/can client beside it receives the same N, python-can's TX and a WS TX land on PCAN, both bridges pumped the same chunk count (the `can` jack fan-out), 0 drops at the server, the bridges and the controller.
