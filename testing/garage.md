# The garage story with the ESPNetLink

Command: `python tools\testbench\wifi\espnetlink_garage_bench.py [--mode usb_rndis\|wifi_modem] [--vpn A\|B\|none] [--only g1,...] [--console auto\|COMx\|none] [--psu auto\|none]` (= `.\test.ps1 garage` for the default run)

Needs: PC + rpi001 (the P4 daemon up, `wican-bench-usb` active) + betty (ssh, `sudo wg`) + the PSU + the dongle on the connector

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**The garage story with the ESPNetLink** (→ `ESPNL GARAGE PASS`, or `ESPNL GARAGE PARTIAL PASS (legs: ...)` with `--only` or a leg skipped for a rig reason; ~35 min; 2026-10-12, Ali: the car in the garage on the home WiFi, away on the dongle's LTE with the VPN, home again, with edge cases). Observed over the DUT's HTTP API: through the Pi tunnel at home, THROUGH THE BETTY TUNNEL (10.9.0.2) while away (nothing else reaches it there), the log ring from the same place; the serial console is optional (probed with `espnetlink`; only g10 `usb vbus` needs it). The default route is proven at a receiver on betty by the source address of every POST: the `garage_pub` row (to betty's public address) arrives from the home side at home and from the carrier away, the `garage_tun` row (to 10.9.0.1) from the DUT's tunnel address; addresses are never printed. Legs: g1 PSU cold boot at home (the station within 60 s, the dongle paired per mode, GPS valid within 180 s, the VPN paused with the home network marked Pause VPN (variant A) or connected (B)); g2 `ap off` (the tunnel reaches the DUT within 180 s; no station and the wire as the uplink, or the station on the dongle's AP in wifi_modem; time still synced; DNS from the dongle); g3 the VPN over LTE (a fresh handshake, the endpoint off home, tun and pub POSTs from away, the transfer counter growing, `/api/vpn` connected, the tun row delivering); g6 `POST /api/espnetlink/repair` through the tunnel (back within 240 s, `vbus_cycles` grew, no WiCAN restart, GPS again); g10 (console only) `usb vbus 0` / `1`; g4 `ap on` (home within 120 s, the pub POSTs from home again = the default route is WiFi, the VPN paused again (A) or the endpoint back home (B), the dongle released (wifi_modem) or the wire idle (rndis)); g5 three 20 s flaps; g7 sleep away / wake at home and g8 sleep at home / wake away (PSU 12.5 / 14.0 V, `sleep_delay_min` 1 for the legs, the current gates relative to the awake reading); g9 (opt-in) the home PSK changed on the P4 (`ap set`, restored from the Pi's `wican-bench` profile, never shown, home again within the ban window); g11 (opt-in) a phone on the WiCAN AP (`apsta` for the run); g12 restore (settings with blank secrets, `wg_public_soak.sh down --wipe`, the receiver, the host route). Rig facts: the P4 daemon must be up (starting it resets the board, and a station kicked by `ap off` can sit in a zombie association: cycle `ap off` / `ap on` and WAIT for `stations: 1`); the Pi gets a host route to betty for the run; `usb_host_manager.prefer_usb_route` must be false (the station holds the default route at home).
