# A tester states the bit rate on a silent bus

Command: `.\test.ps1 j2534auto` (= `python tools/testbench/usb/j2534_auto_baud_bench.py --dut localhost:18081 --j2534 localhost:18534 --slcan localhost:18333`; by hand through your own tunnel of ports 80, 6809 and 3333)

Needs: PCAN on the DUT bus + the simulator answering 7DF + rpi001

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**A tester states the bit rate on a silent bus** (prints `J2534 AUTO BAUD PASS`, ~4 min, 3 planned restarts; the second half of the Discord report of 2026-10-17: "J2534 driver works, communication established, no comm to the canbus", reproduced as `can_manager.baud = auto` on the bench bus, which like a gatewayed OBD port speaks only when asked: CONNECT fine, every WRITE `ERR_FAILED`, the node detecting and listen-only for ever). The rule since: `can_ab_adopt()` takes a bitrate a tester states when `auto` has nothing proven, the bus has been silent 300 ms at the candidate in use and was never bitten; the node runs there in the configured mode, `adopted` and unverified, until frames prove it or the watchdog demotes it; a fixed bit rate is never overruled. Leg C configures (j2534 on with allow_lan, `br_slcan` beside the monitor's bridge, `auto`, autopid's group paused; one restart). Leg R: CONNECT(CAN, 20000) on the detecting node, not a rate it knows, opens the channel, the WRITE answers `ERR_FAILED`, nothing reaches the bus, `/api/j2534` `tx_refused` steps, the ring gains `W j2534_server: CAN write refused` and `W can_core: transmit refused: the node may not talk`. Leg A: CONNECT(CAN, 500000) is taken (`/api/can` running, `adopted`, not verified, not listen-only, the ring's `bitrate stated by the tester`), the WRITE 7DF goes out (PCAN) and the simulator's `06 41 00` comes back as RX_MSG, two PCAN frames then prove the rate (`verified`, `baud_detected` 500), ISO15765 CONNECT + FLOW_CONTROL + `10 02` answers `50 02`, a CONNECT at 250000 is `ERR_INVALID_BAUDRATE` now. Leg S: after a plain restart (detecting again) an slcan client's `S6\rO\r` through `br_slcan` is taken the same way and its `t7DF` reaches the bus. Leg X: settings as found (one restart), planned restarts only, E lines and unexpected resets unchanged. Host rules: `test.ps1 host can_manager` (two `can_ab_adopt` tests, 46 PASS) and `translator_slcan` (the `S` hook, 13 PASS).
