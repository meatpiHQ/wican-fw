# OBD chip sleep triggers switched off: the stored thresholds have no effect

Command: `.\test.ps1 sleeptrig [-UartSilence]` = `python tools\testbench\obd\obd_sleep_triggers_bench.py --dut localhost:18081 --psu-port auto [--uart-silence 25] [--log-dir DIR]` (PC, the DUT through the tunnel, the ECU sim v2 supply, the console tailed beside it)

Needs: main fw on DUT + rpi001 + the ECU sim v2 supply (over its USB network link). Sleep enabled with its default thresholds (the device must stay awake through the 12.5 V leg: 190 s against the 5 min countdown; the critical floor at 11.9 V is never approached). AutoPID and every setting are left as found.

The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md). The question it answers: `internal/OBD_CHIP_SLEEP_V6_VS_V4.md` section 7 (Ali, 2026-11-07: "do we need to set this voltage? maybe not read them from configuration"), and the vendor's PowerSave text (OBDLink 8.11 and 15.2: "each trigger can be independently enabled or disabled").

## What it covers

The bring-up provisions the chip's sleep configuration from the obd_chip settings: `STSLVLW >wake, 1` and `STSLVLS <sleep, time` (the voltage thresholds), `STSLUIT 1200` (the UART inactivity timeout), and then switches the triggers that would act on them OFF (`STSLVl off,off`, `STSLU off, off`): the ESP32 owns the sleep policy through `sleep_manager` and never arms the chip's own. Before the stored values are dropped from the provisioning (fewer EEPROM writes, no re-provision when an API-only number changes), this stage proves on the chip itself that they do nothing while the controls are off.

**The change the stage guards (2026-11-07, Ali: "let's do the proposed change").** The bring-up no longer writes `STSLVLW`, `STSLVLS` or `STSLUIT` and no longer compares the chip's stored thresholds with the obd_chip settings (`wake_voltage_mv`, `sleep_voltage_mv`, `sleep_time_min`: kept in the schema as reserved, never shown in the UI): `obd_stslcs_needs_provision()` looks at the four control states only, and a provisioning writes `STSLVl off,off`, `STSLU off, off` and the PP 0F pair, then `ATZ`. Whatever a chip holds as thresholds stays as it is.

## Traps

- The PSU's first `*IDN?` can go unanswered (the known false negative); the bench opens it with three attempts.
- The 12.5 V leg runs under the device's own sleep threshold: with the default 5 min delay the leg's 170 s leaves margin, with a short delay set on the DUT the leg aborts itself at 45 s before the device would sleep. The critical floor (11.9 V, 5 min) is never approached.
- The chip asleep by command with PPSW `2F` wakes on nothing our sequence does: the planned restart is what brings it back (the bring-up finds READY asleep and pulses the reset), and the same boot repairs PPSW. Two EEPROM writes of that cell per run.
- `ATRV` over the wire probe is the chip's own measurement (its divider reads a little under the PSU); the thresholds compare against that reading, not the PSU's setpoint.
