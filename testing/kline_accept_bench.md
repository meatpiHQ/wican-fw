# A K-line car through the firmware's own paths, and the other pre-CAN protocols

Command: `.\test.ps1 kline [-Detect]` (PC; it opens the tunnel and runs `python tools\testbench\obd\kline_accept_bench.py [--dut localhost:18081] [--only test,others,pin,poll] [--detect]`)

Needs: main fw on DUT + rpi001 + ECU sim v2 with its K-line and J1850 buses wired to the DUT's OBD connector, Automate off at the start. Prints `KLINE ACCEPT PASS`. About 6.5 min, three planned restarts of the DUT (Automate on with protocol 3 pinned, protocol 2 pinned, and back), about ten restarts of the simulator, no write of the OBD chip. With `-Detect`: about 1 min more, two more restarts, and two writes of the chip's stored protocol (the one save a detection makes by design, there and back). The unit's tables, its Automate settings, the vehicle store and the simulator are put back as found.

The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md). The host half: `.\test.ps1 host obd_chip` (`test_kline.c`, 7 cases) and `.\test.ps1 host autopid` (`test_precan.c`, 7 cases), [host.md](host.md). The page: `node tools\webui_preview\probe_automate.mjs` (the ten options of the protocol setting, named).

## What it covers

GitHub issue #1045: a 2000 Toyota 4Runner and a 2005 Porsche Cayman S, both ISO 9141-2. An ELM app read the car through the OBD chip; the firmware's own paths did not: the Test button failed, the detection found no ECU, and a poll never got an answer in time. ECU sim v2 plays that car (its K-line ECU alone, 5 baud init, every CAN bus off), and its own count of bus starts (`kline.inits_slow`) is the witness that the firmware does not start the bus again between two requests. Since 2026-11-12 the poll leg also scans the car's trouble codes: these protocols carry three codes a message, and a car with more than three showed three.

## Traps

- **Automate must be off for the leg "others".** Its first run came behind the poll leg, with Automate still on and the protocol pinned to 3: between two Test calls the poller took the chip back to protocol 3 (and failed there, the simulator being another car), so every call started its bus again and three checks were red. The leg now runs before Automate is switched on.
- **A Test call that starts a K-line bus takes about 2.7 s** (0.6 s with the fast init). Give the HTTP call more than the usual 1.5 s.
- **The Test route answers 200 with `ok` true when nobody is there**: `raw` is `BUS INIT: ...ERROR` after 2.8 s, `error` is `no payload in response`. Judge by the value or the reply bytes.
- **The simulator's K-line ECU carries the same VIN as its CAN ECU.** A detection on the K-line car is therefore a detection of the bench unit's own car on another protocol: its record moves to protocol 3 and the chip's store with it. The detect leg takes both back the same way; do not run a detection against the K-line car by hand without that way back, the app leg of `.\test.ps1 multibus` and every ELM app expect 6 in the chip's store.
- **One protocol save per boot.** The way back needs a restart between the two detections, or the second save is put off (`chip protocol save deferred`).
- **The job's `trying` changes faster than a page polls.** A CAN try with nobody on the bus takes well under a second; the bench looks four times a second and still misses some. It checks where the job stopped, not every step.
- **The simulator's trouble codes are not a setting.** `POST /api/ecu/dtcs` changes the running list (`bench_bus.sim_dtcs()`); its restart (every `sim_only()`) loads the list of its profile again (P0300 and P0420 stored, P0171 pending). Set the list behind the bus change, not ahead of it.
- **A simulator with a firmware before v1.00-16 (2026-11-13) sends each ISO 14230 answer in one long message** (the code list with a count, the VIN whole), where the standard gives a car seven data bytes a message. Against it the leg "others" is red on its two ISO 14230-4 checks (one message where two and five are expected), and a firmware that reads only the first message passes the scan there. Update the simulator.
- **The simulator restarts when its buses are changed** (about 8 s each); the leg "others" costs five of them. The simulator is the unit's supply and keeps it up through its own restart.
