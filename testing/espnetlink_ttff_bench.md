# Cold GPS time-to-first-fix series on the ESPNetLink

Command: `python tools\testbench\wifi\espnetlink_ttff_bench.py [--n 6] [--bench-ap off\|keep]`

Needs: PSU + rpi001 (wtest1 free, `espnl-client` with the dongle's key) + the paired dongle on the WiCAN's connector + the P4 bench AP daemon for `--bench-ap off`

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**Cold GPS time-to-first-fix series on the ESPNetLink** (prints `TTFF SERIES [...]`, **2026-10-12: one series per WiCAN mode is owed for the record; the first cycle in `usb_rndis` on unit 894AA5 (the wire live, the new firmware on both sides) gave LTE at 29 s and NO fix within 300 s (271 polls), the known desense with the USB link live, and the series then hung: the Pi's poller printed its result but did not exit and the bench waited on it (open; the Pi poller records `usb_ethernet` instead of `boot_cut` since that day)**; the median and `ESPNL TTFF PASS` when at least half the cycles fixed and the median is inside the campaign envelope of 121 s; ~3 to 30 min for six cycles). The August measurement as a repo bench (it lived in session scratchpads before and was lost): PSU output off for 10 s (the WiCAN and the bus-powered dongle fully dead), t0 = `OUTP 1`, the Pi's free radio joins the dongle's AP (`espnl-client` must carry the dongle's current key) and polls `/api/gps` + `/api/wifi_modem` once a second until 30 s after the first fix (cap 300 s); the Pi poller is `tools/testbench/pi/pi_ttff_poll.py`, deployed by scp each run. `--bench-ap off` (default) parks the P4 bench AP for the series so the WiCAN falls back to the dongle's AP and polls it every 2 s exactly like the 2026-08-24/25 baseline (boot-cut steady state: zero USB contact after the first pairing), and restores it at the end. Per cycle: `tfix` (s after PSU on), `tfix_uptime` (the dongle's own uptime at that poll, immune to the PC/Pi clock offset, which was 129 s on 2026-10-09), satellites, fixpct over the window, when LTE connected and its RSSI; results and per-second samples land in `test-reports/logs/<label>*.jsonl`.
