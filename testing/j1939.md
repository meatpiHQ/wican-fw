# The J1939 listener, autopid on it, and active mode on native CAN

Command: `.\test.ps1 j1939`

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**The J1939 listener, autopid on it, and active mode on native CAN** (`tools/testbench/can/j1939_bench.py` → `J1939 PASS`, ~3 min, 4 DUT restarts; then `tools/testbench/can/j1939_conservation_test.py` → `J1939 CONSERVATION PASS`, ~9 min, 3 restarts; `TASK_j1939_wwh.md` phase 4). The truck is the PCAN actor (`actors/pcan_j1939_truck.py`, codec `lib/j1939_ref.py`, cross-checked against a J1939 DBC); the ECU simulator only acknowledges (its own ECU off, `enabled=false` after a restart AT the test bitrate). The DUT listens with `can_manager` on `auto` + `silent` and `j1939` enabled; the benches stage that and restore everything. Listener legs: `j1` every value of the built-in table equals what the truck sends (21), every stored payload equals the truck's bytes (16 groups), the VIN arrives by BAM, two sources of one group are kept apart and the lowest address is the pick, periods are measured, the transport and frame books balance, the DUT's `tx` stays 0 and the adapter sees no frame but its own, the OBD chip is untouched; `j2` DM1 with three codes by BAM (lamps, occurrence counts), one code in a frame, none; `j3` the truck falls silent: ages grow, nothing changes, nothing is invented; `j4` not available and error raw values carry no value; `j5` a BAM with a dropped, a reordered and a missing packet: nothing stored, each failure in its counter; `j6` the bus moves to 500k and is found again; `j7` can_manager in normal mode (the DUT acknowledges): still not one frame from it; `j8` j1939 enabled with the native bus off: state `no_bus`, one warning, no error. Conservation: exactly N (20000) frames of four keys at 1000 frames/s, 80 % load and line rate, 250k and 500k, twice each: every key's count moves by its share, `/api/can` rx and the listener's `rx_frames` by N, every loss counter (`rx_overrun`, `rx_missed`, `queue_drops`, `not_kept`) 0 (at line rate a loss passes only when those counters name every frame of it); then 80 % load while every status route a page polls is requested ten times a second, and while a 2 KB file is written to /data twice a second: exact. Both fail on an unexpected reset, a new fault, an `E` line of their tags, a task under 512 B of stack headroom or a heap floor under 20 KB.

**2026-10-03: PASS** (98 checks; all values 1.0 s after the truck started, the 500k bus found again in 1.0 s; line rate 1786 frames/s at 250k, 3545 at 500k, 0 frames lost). The conservation bench's `while_polled` leg is what found that `GET /api/status` and `GET /api/status/tasks` cost CAN frames (interrupts off for 3 to 6 ms: a heap walk and the kernel's task snapshot) before 2026-10-03.

**Third stage (2026-10-03, phase 5): `tools/testbench/obd/autopid_j1939_bench.py` → `AUTOPID J1939 PASS`** (~10 min, 7 restarts; the obd0 ELM bridge tunnelled too): the truck (PCAN, a twin source, three active codes) runs before the DUT boots; `a1` detection with the native bus OFF samples the bus (`dialect j1939`, `j1939_listening false`, `bus_kbps 250`, the truck stored by its controllers with the rows of the groups heard); `a2` the listener on: first contact learns the VIN from the broadcast without a detection job, every stored row carries the truck's value, the chip's counters stay 0, its UART sends nothing, `rx_diag` stays put, `/api/can tx` 0; `a3` a change on the truck at the API and on the Pi's broker; `a4` an ELM app on the bridge (ATSP9, hinted 010C loop): `paused_client` while the PGN rows keep publishing; `a5` `pause_mode` round trip; `a6` the DTC report is the DM1 (protocol `j1939`, lamps, `sa` + `oc` per item, the clear 403 on a listener); `a7` test-a-PID from the store, a pinned source reads the twin, a pinned custom row, `init` on a PGN row refused; `a8` silence: nothing new published, values keep their reading and time and grow old.
