Claims: every request reached the ECU and was answered (no flow-control timeout), every filter parameter equals what its expression means on the frame data, every PID parameter equals what its expression means in the legacy frame of reference the published file uses (every byte after the CAN header, PCI bytes included: `parse_elm327_response()` in the legacy main/autopid.c), the rows keep being re-read, the standard rows keep reading the simulator beside the profile, then every PID through the test route (the chip's raw reply lines go to the log) and restore (tables, settings, `0100` answered by the simulator). A parameter past the 256-byte frames cap (`AP_FRAMES_MAX`) is reported as a warning, not failed.

**First runs 2026-10-21 (firmware alfa.7-2-gb0fca3a): the three filters PASS** (SPEED, the signed DM_F_RPM and ODOMETER decoded exactly from 0x284 / 0x1DA / 0x5C5 broadcast every 50 ms), every request answered 20 times in 60 s, flow control 80 of 80, `polls_failed` +0, the standard rows kept reading RPM 800, restore clean; **the four multi-frame PIDs FAIL and the two single-frame ones pass**, two defects: (1) the importer shifts every index down by one, right for a single frame (`0L 41 05 xx`) and wrong for a multi-frame reply, whose published indexes count the first frame's two PCI bytes and one per consecutive frame while the chip hands v6 the PCI-free rows (`037 / 0: 61 01 .. / 1: ..`): 21018, 2103 and 21045 decode exactly the shifted expression on the PCI-free payload; (2) a reply over 16 rows is assembled in the wrong order: the chip prints row indexes 0..F and wraps, `assemble_headerless()` orders the rows by that printed index, so rows 16 and up interleave with 0 and up: the 28-row 2102 decodes exactly the shifted expression on that re-ordered payload (54 of 54 polled values, 84 of 84 from the test route; the Toyota bZ4X `22181A2` and the Xpeng `221122` are the two other published replies over 16 rows).
