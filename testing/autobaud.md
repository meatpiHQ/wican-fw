# CAN listen before talk, auto bit rate and the autopid bus guard

Command: `.\test.ps1 autobaud`

Needs: PCAN on the DUT's bus + ECU sim v2 (its REST over the USB network link) + rpi001 (tunnel)

**The PCAN's plugs may be swapped.** The stage talks on the adapter's channel 1, which belongs on the simulator's DB9 J2 (the DUT's bus); channel 2 belongs on J3. If the run is red from `c0_bus_healthy` on (3 of 600 frames on the bus, thousands of error frames, and a `NOTE:` line from the bench), channel 1 is not on the DUT's bus: swap the plugs back, or run on channel 2 with `$env:WICAN_PCAN_DUT = 'PCAN_USBBUS2'` ahead of `.\test.ps1 autobaud`. Nothing below that check means anything in such a run, and its detections can move the OBD chip's stored protocol (2026-11-13; "Troubleshooting" in [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md) says how to tell which channel is where).
