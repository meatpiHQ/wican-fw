# CAN listen before talk, auto bit rate and the autopid bus guard

Command: `.\test.ps1 autobaud`

Needs: PCAN on the DUT's bus + the ECU simulator (USB-NCM 192.168.8.1) + rpi001 (tunnel)

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**CAN listen before talk, auto bit rate and the autopid bus guard** (`tools/testbench/can/can_autobaud_bench.py` → `CAN AUTOBAUD PASS`, ~9 min, 18 DUT restarts, 2 simulator restarts; `TASK_j1939_wwh.md` phase 2). The claim: the DUT never destroys the traffic of a bus it cannot read, with its native controller or through the OBD chip, and it finds the bit rate of a live bus by listening. The JUDGE is the wire: the PCAN adapter keeps the bus alive (29-bit id 18FF1021, 200 frames/s), the ECU simulator at the same bit rate is the ACK source, and the adapter counts the bus errors it sees across every restart and in an 8 s steady window (PCAN reports a destroyed frame as one bus error plus eight counter-change messages with id 0: only the first kind counts). 18 legs: `n1`..`n4` on a 500k bus (500 normal runs; 250 normal and 250 silent read `mismatch` and destroy nothing; `auto` detects 500), `n5` the bus moves to 250k under the running auto node (demoted, re-detected), `n6`..`n8` on 250k (500 normal / silent: `mismatch`, nothing destroyed; 250 normal runs clean), `n9` auto on a silent bus stays listen-only until traffic names the bit rate, `n10` a fixed bit rate on a silent bus runs as configured, is demoted when traffic at another bit rate starts and stays listen-only after, `n11` auto + silent reads the adapter at every bit rate of the setting (125, 1000, 100, 83.3, 95.2, 33.3, 500, 250), `n12` traffic at 800k: every candidate tried, state `mismatch`, no bit rate claimed, `n13` the same bouncing node with `GET /api/can` asked from four threads for a minute: no reset (until 2026-10-05 a status read on a node the link policy was deleting loaded through a NULL register pointer, about one panic in 150 s of this: the reset of the EU truck bench's first run, TASK phase 7 addendum, finding 28); then the chip: `c0` 500k live with protocol Automatic polls, `c1` 250k live with protocol 6 pinned is parked before the first request (zero bytes to the chip, detection / std scan / test-a-PID / DTC scan / DTC clear answer 409 with the guard's sentence), `c2` Automatic with a stored protocol 6 on a 250k bus uses the chip's search and the store learns 8, `c3` silent at boot then 250k traffic: parked at the first look that reads frames, `c4` silent at boot then 800k traffic: parked as unreadable, polling resumes when it stops. Restores the simulator, the vehicle store's protocol and every setting; fails on an unexpected reset or an `E` line of its own tags (`/api/logs/ring`). `--only n5,c3` runs chosen legs (verdict `CAN AUTOBAUD PARTIAL PASS`). The stage opens its own ssh tunnel to the DUT (port 18081); by hand: `python tools/testbench/can/can_autobaud_bench.py localhost:8081`.

**2026-10-03: PASS** (93 checks; demotion cost 4 and 5 frames in `n5` and `n10`, re-detection 73 ms, detection from silence 74 ms, chip parked 231 ms after the first 250k frame at a cost of 0, parked on unreadable traffic after 1.2 s; 0 unexpected resets). The first gate run that day FAILED with thousands of bus errors in every 250k leg and found that a DUT restart did not reset the OBD chip (it kept an open 500k session; fixed in `obd_chip_bringup.c`). Bench facts: the simulator cannot leave the bus (its CAN controller survives its software restart and keeps ACKing at its last bit rate, `enabled=false` or not), so the legs at other bit rates run with the DUT listen-only.

**2026-10-03, the interrupt watchdog**: two of five runs of the day rebooted the DUT in `n11` (the walk reaching 83 / 95 kbit/s; `no_unexpected_reset` and `n11_reads_95k` failed; the first of them had been filed as a deep-sleep wake by restart_tracker's IDF 4 reset numbers); reproduced with the console tailed on the third `--only n11` run: `Interrupt wdt timeout on CPU0`, `_xt_lowint1` busy for 300 ms over an idle task = a TWAI bus-error interrupt storm (a 1000 kbit/s candidate listening to a slow bus: a bit error every few bit times, `rx_bad` +134 989 in 4 s) starving the tick. Fixed in `can_core_driver.c` (the error ISR masks the controller's interrupts past 20 000 errors/s, the link policy bounces the deaf node); `n11` ran four times clean with the console on afterwards, 10 storms throttled, the leg's `rx_bad` down to +1300; the bench asserts `n11_error_storms_throttled` since (`rx_storms` on `/api/can`).
