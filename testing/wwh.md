# OBD over UDS (ISO 27145 WWH-OBD, SAE J1979-2) as an autopid dialect

Command: `.\test.ps1 wwh`

Moved from the `TESTING.md` test-kinds table on 2026-10-31 (the row's detail cell, whole, as paragraphs). The index row and the other stages: [../TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

**OBD over UDS (ISO 27145 WWH-OBD, SAE J1979-2) as an autopid dialect** (`tools/testbench/obd/wwh_obd_bench.py` → `WWH OBD PASS`, ~7 min, 12 DUT restarts, 8 simulator restarts; `TASK_j1939_wwh.md` phase 3). The claim: such a vehicle is detected, polled, its trouble codes read and cleared with no step an OBD-II car would not need, and the device tells the two kinds apart by itself when it is moved between them. The VEHICLE is the PCAN adapter (`actors/pcan_wwh_ecu.py`, real ISO-TP, one `ECU {json}` log line per request: which ECU, functional or physical, what it answered), so half of the checks are the wire's word; the ECU simulator stays on the bus as the ACK source with its own ECU off, and is the OBD-II car of the last leg. Ten legs: `w1` a vehicle the store has never seen (29-bit, 500k, two ECUs) is found by first contact with nobody asking: protocol 7, `dialect` uds, VIN, both ECUs with their bitmaps, 17 rows each addressed to its owner (`init: ATSH18DA00F1`, F43C to `3D`), expressions one byte further in; `w2` every value equals what the actor sends, each row is asked physically of its owner and nobody else is asked, no failed poll; `w3` a custom row with no init between the standard rows goes out with the FUNCTIONAL header (both ECUs see it); `w4` DTC scan: codes of both ECUs with status and severity, the lamp per ECU, the permanent code, `7F 19 78` ridden out twice per answer, the clear refused (403) with nothing sent while `dtc_allow_clear` is off, polling undisturbed; `w5` after a restart the known vehicle is back without a detection, the clear sends `14 FF FF 33` to both ECUs, codes gone, lamp off, the permanent code stays; `w6` the same codes in the SAE J1939 DTC format (`SPN3226-4`); `w7` an 11-bit vehicle with one ECU and no F810: protocol 6, rows addressed to `7E0`; `w8` the Mercedes Sprinter VS30 of the field report (`--sprinter`: ECUs 58 / 59 / 5D with the reported bitmaps, 763 rpm from 58): no row for a PID that decodes nothing, ECU `5A` turns up later with an unscaled copy of the engine speed and is never asked, the next boot meets four ECUs and it is still the same van; `w9` a 250k bus that is live at boot: the bus guard names the bitrate, the vehicle is found on protocol 9 and the adapter sees ZERO bus errors; `w10` back on the OBD-II simulator with nobody asking, then that car on 29-bit ids: its ECU is told apart (`18DAF110`) and a DTC scan reads it (it found no ECU before 2026-10-03). Restores the store (the bench's vehicles forgotten), the tables, every setting and the simulator; fails on an unexpected reset, a new fault or an `E` line of its own tags. After every job, before every restart and after every leg it also reads what the device only says in passing: the stack headroom the detection job, the vehicle writer and the DTC job log when they end, the poller's from the task list and the internal heap's floor (METRIC lines; under 512 B or under 20 KB fails, 2026-10-03: detection job 2948 B, vehicle writer 3108 B, poller 9368 B, DTC job 14200 B, heap floor 66895 B). `--only w4,w5` runs chosen legs after `w1` (verdict `WWH OBD PARTIAL PASS`). The stage opens its own ssh tunnel to the DUT (port 18081) and keeps the actor's logs in `test-reports/logs/`; by hand: `python tools/testbench/obd/wwh_obd_bench.py localhost:8081`.

**2026-10-03: PASS** (59 checks; first value of a new vehicle 17.6 s after boot, of a known one 10.5 s, on the live 250k bus 17.8 s, after the switch back to the OBD-II car 13.7 s; 16.5 polls a second on 17 rows at a 1 s period). Traps: the simulator's CAN controller survives its restart, so `enabled=false` keeps it on the bus at the bitrate it LAST ran at (the 250k leg goes through `250k, on` first); never kill the actor (the PEAK driver wedges): the bench asks it to stop through `--stop-file`.
