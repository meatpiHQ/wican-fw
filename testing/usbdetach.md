**A warm restart in the USB device role drops off the bus first** (prints `USB DETACH PASS`, ~6 min, 1 + `--restarts` planned restarts; 2026-10-17, `internal/TASK_usb_detach.md`, from the ESPNetLink bench of 2026-10-16). The claim: with the DUT a USB NCM device to this PC, every planned restart (`POST /api/restart`, a settings submit, an OTA apply) is seen by the PC as an unplug within 5 s of the request (measured 2.2 to 2.4 s) (usb_host_manager's esp_restart shutdown handler sets the dwc2 soft-disconnect bit), the device comes back as a fresh enumeration, the link answers, and the next boot is a plain `software` reset; without the detach the host is never told and a transfer stays in flight across the reset (the dongle crashed on the stale completion). Legs: d0 as found; d1 the device role (one planned restart when it was host), the PC enumerates the DUT by its USB serial, a NET adapter binds, `/api/info` over 192.168.82.1, the DUT reports `ncm-dev`, the internal heap at `--settle`; d2 the restarts: the path to the DUT gone within `--detach-max` (a TCP connect at 10 Hz from a socket bound to the PC's own address on the link: the bind fails at once while the adapter is gone; a plain connect would only time out, the PC's default route absorbs the address), the device back (PnP), the link within 40 s, the record `software` / `user_request` / no crash; d3 the heap again within 4 KB of d1, no E line of the USB tags, the `usb_net_dev` headroom, the heap floor; d4 the restore. The PC's PnP view comes from one long-lived PowerShell (0.25 s a poll; a CIM query can block across a whole absence, which is why the probe, not PnP, times the detach). An RNDIS leg cannot bind on this PC (Windows 25H2's inbox driver takes only the MS OS compat id; the bridge presents E0/01/03): SKIP with the reason.
