**Raw CAN forwards whatever the tool sends: the VW TP 2.0 frame shapes** (prints `TP20 RAW CAN PASS`, 13 checks, ~75 s, 2 planned restarts). The Discord report of 2026-10-27 (elias: a custom flasher over KWP2000 on TP 2.0 got `PassThruWriteMsgs: NotSupported (0x01)` "randomly", `GetLastError` "no error") was reproduced byte-exact that morning with the bench's first form (`TP20 GATE REPRODUCED`, `test-reports/2026-10-27_j2534_tp20_notsupported_repro.md`): `can_reflash_blocked()` read every raw CAN frame as ISO-TP (nibble 0 = a single frame, byte 1 the service; nibble 1 = a first frame, byte 2 the service) and refused `0x34..0x37` while `allow_reflash` was off, and a TP 2.0 data frame starts `0x0N`/`0x1N` (a sequence counter) with payload behind. The gate was removed the same day (no content gate on any channel, `allow_reflash` gone, settings v2) and the bench became the regression. Leg T: a TP 2.0 session's ordinary frames all pass. Leg S: the sweep `0x1N 00 xx`, `0x0N xx` over xx 0..255 and `0x2N`/`0x3N` samples (three sequence numbers): nothing refused, every frame on the bus (PCAN), the short forms `03 34` / `13 00 36` pass, no gate line in the device log, `/api/j2534` `reflash_seen` unchanged (raw CAN is not inspected). Leg D: the installed DLL passes the two frames the old gate refused (`GATE FRAME OK`, status 0x0) and the ordinary ones (`j2534_tp20_dll_check.py`), plus the write round trip and the 7DF request/answer turnaround over the path in use. Leg X: settings as found, planned restarts only, no new E lines
