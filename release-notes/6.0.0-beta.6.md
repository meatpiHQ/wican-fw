# WiCAN PRO 6.0.0-beta.6

Supported device: [WiCAN PRO](https://www.crowdsupply.com/meatpi-electronics/wican-pro) only. Home Assistant: v6 requires the [meatpi-multi-device](https://github.com/meatpiHQ/ha-wican/tree/meatpi-multi-device) branch of the ha-wican integration.

## Before you update

<!-- /release.py:layout -->
- Coming from a v6 alfa or beta: settings, profiles and files are kept; the standard PID rows are re-numbered at the first start. A custom PID you wrote on a v6 alfa or beta against a single-frame reply reads one byte too far: run Test a PID (B0 is the first byte after the CAN id) and move each B index up by one, or re-import the published profile.
- A beta.1 device that parked itself (LED breathing red) updates from safe mode: hold the button until the LED turns sky blue, then yellow, join the device's own access point (its name starts with `WiCAN_`, password `@meatpi#`), open `http://192.168.0.10` and upload the firmware there. Settings are kept.
- Coming from firmware 4.x: the stored files, logs and the saved configuration are erased by the new partition layout; download a backup first (System > Maintenance > Configuration backup). The device restarts once more on its first start.
- In case of a boot loop, perform a [factory reset](https://meatpihq.github.io/wican-fw/factory-reset/factory-reset).

## New since beta.5
- System > Maintenance has an **OBD chip** card: the chip's state and firmware, a warning when the chip is still on V2.3.22 while the device carries V2.3.24, what the chip answered when it did not come up, "Read the chip configuration" (identity, voltage, protocol, programmable parameters and sleep settings as one block to paste into a bug report), wire tools for a chip that will not come up, and "Rewrite the chip firmware" behind a confirmation.
- A **CAN self-test** card beside it: the OBD chip asks for engine speed the way every OBD app does and the WiCAN's own CAN controller answers it as an engine ECU over the shared bus, so one exchange proves both transceivers, both directions and the wiring, with or without a vehicle. The result is one block to copy. Afterwards the chip is put back as the start-up left it and AutoPID re-sends its set-up, so polling carries on as before.
- The Maintenance card (Reboot, Restart History, Run Quick Setup again, Factory Reset) now leads the Maintenance page.
- Console and API: `obd_chip` (state, version, `report`, `update [--force]`, `probe [baud] [scan|auto] [word]`, `listen`), `can_selftest run`; `GET /api/obd_chip` with `fw.outdated`, `/api/obd_chip/report`, `/api/obd_chip/probe`, `/api/obd_chip/update`, `/api/can/selftest`.

## Bug fixes since beta.5
- Fixed the OBD chip's firmware being downgraded: v6 shipped chip firmware V2.3.22 and rewrote any chip not on it, so a WiCAN PRO that firmware 4.52 had taken to V2.3.24 was written back 3557 records at its first v6 start, and a chip left in its download mode by an interrupted write could not be recovered from the device. The packaged chip firmware is V2.3.24 and, as in 4.52, a chip on V2.3.22 or V2.3.24 is left alone (V2.3.22 is flagged on the card). A chip that does not answer in the normal way is probed at both UART speeds, what it sends is logged, and the recovery download is attempted as 4.x did; only a silent chip is left untouched. The sleep entry waits for a running chip update (#1023).
- Fixed the ESPNetLink's APN, username, password and SIM PIN set on the WiCAN (Settings > USB > ESPNetLink, or the Quick Setup) never reaching the dongle in the default USB Ethernet mode: the dongle kept its own APN (`internet`), a SIM that needs another one (a Simbase SIM needs `simbase`) never attached, and the page still said the APN was on the dongle. The settings travel with the dongle's USB sharing settings and are applied by the one dongle restart the pairing makes. WiFi modem mode was not affected.
- Fixed the Test button, the vehicle detection and the trouble-code jobs answering "chip busy" when they started while a passive filter was listening on the bus; they wait for that window now.

## New since beta.4
- The Quick Setup's vehicle step asks which car it is before the detection: a published profile from the list, or no profile and detection as before. With a profile, the car is stored under the profile's name, the detection pins the protocol the profile expects, and a car that answers no standard request (a Nissan Leaf) is confirmed by the profile's own request. A car seen without its VIN shows "No VIN", and "Same car as..." folds it into the entry it belongs to.
- A published profile's passive CAN filters come along with its PIDs (the Nissan Leaf ZE1, 2018 onwards, is the first with them: speed, motor RPM and odometer from broadcast frames). Filters made by hand stay; a re-import replaces the profile's own.
- A WiCAN plugged into a car it was not set up for used to show an empty Dashboard without a word; a bar under the header and a banner on the Dashboard now say so and lead to the vehicle step.
- Factory reset returns the device to its factory state: settings, stored vehicles and tables, trouble-code databases, DBC files, scripts, the dashboard, certificate sets, the backup copy on the internal flash and the NVS records, from the web UI, the console (`factoryreset`) and safe mode alike. Kept: the crash report and the backup key, so the copy on the SD card still opens; the SD card is never touched. Until now only the settings were erased.
- J2534: what a tool sends is no longer inspected; reprogramming services (0x34 to 0x37) are forwarded and counted (`reflash_seen`). The "Allow ECU flashing" switch is gone; enabling the J2534 server is the consent.
- J2534 Windows driver 1.5.0: event-driven reads, the driver log on by default (Public Documents), PassThruGetLastError names every refusal.
- "USB link address" on the USB page (default 192.168.82.1) for PCs whose own network sits on that subnet.
- Built with ESP-IDF v6.1 (the betas before it: v6.0.2); about 4 KB more internal RAM free after boot. The boot console prints the lowest internal RAM point of the boot (`WICAN RAMMIN`).

## Bug fixes since beta.4
- Fixed a vehicle without a readable VIN that could not be forgotten or given a profile ("Car xxxx", Forget answered "no such route"): the browser percent-encodes the car's `fp:` key and the device did not decode it. Such an entry appears when a car answers no VIN at one contact and its VIN at another; the two are kept apart on purpose, and the extra entry can be forgotten.
- Fixed an empty command sent to the OBD chip (answered `?`) in the start-up sequence on K-line and J1850 vehicles and when no vehicle is current: the headers-off command was dropped by the filter meant for the user's own init strings. The no-vehicle sequence now sets headers on explicitly.
- Fixed J2534 PassThruWriteMsgs answering NotSupported at random to VW TP 2.0 flashers: the reprogramming gate read TP 2.0 data frames as ISO-TP. The gate is gone.
- Fixed two MQTT error lines at every planned restart while connected to the broker: the session is closed first.

## New since beta.3
- An SD card the device cannot read is named for what it holds (exFAT, the factory format of every card over 32 GB; NTFS; blank; a damaged boot sector) instead of "Not mounted", on the header bar, the Status page, the File Manager, the Logger and the backup copies card, with **Format the card**.
- Formatting from the web UI writes FAT32 (one partition, 32 KB clusters above 32 GB) after you confirm and type `format sdcard`; a mounted card is never formatted through this path. The console has `sdcard --format --yes`. `GET /api/sdcard` reports the slot; `GET /api/status` carries an `sd` summary.

## Bug fixes since beta.3
- Fixed an unreadable SD card logging three error lines at every insertion and latching a `boot_errors` fault: the card is probed before any mount and reported in one warning line.

## Bug fixes since beta.2
- Fixed multi-frame vehicle profile values decoding as garbage (the Kia Niro EV state of charge and cells, the Nissan Leaf, the BMW i3, the Hyundai and Kia battery rows, the Xpeng and Toyota cell lists): the v6 alfa and beta read the reply with the ISO-TP framing bytes stripped. The device counts the bytes as the published profiles do (#1014).
- Standard PID rows stored by an earlier v6 are re-numbered automatically; Test a PID shows the bytes as the expressions count them; an init chain's ATH0, ATS0 and ATE1 are ignored as the 4.x firmware did.
- Fixed a reply from an ESPNetLink or another USB network device vanishing when its length was a multiple of 64 bytes.
- An ESPNetLink that is plugged in but does not answer is reported as unreachable and retried every 10 s, with one USB power cycle after two minutes.

## Bug fixes since beta.1
- Fixed a crash on every Home Assistant webhook push (a beta.1 device with the integration crashed about 15 s after each start, three times, then parked): the update status read the flash from the webhook task.
- A firmware update no longer freezes the device for about 5 s at its start: the flash is erased as the image arrives.
- An OBD app over the WebSocket channel could connect at the wrong moment and receive nothing; the handshake moment is recognised now.

## New since alfa-06
- Firmware updates from the MeatPi release list: System > Firmware update shows what the selected channel (stable, beta or alfa) offers, with the release notes, and installs it. A new image that crashes three times is rolled back.
- A connected ESPNetLink is updated through the WiCAN PRO, over USB or WiFi (dongle firmware 1.23 or newer). Downloads run over WiFi only, never over the dongle's mobile connection. The dongle's assisted GPS and, in WiFi mode, its access point are switched on by the WiCAN PRO.
- The firmware version reads `6.0.0-beta.N`; the build identifier moved to the About page and `/api/info`.
- J2534: ISO15765 messages follow the SAE J2534-1 layout, one ISO-TP session per flow-control filter, TxDone and start-of-message indications; J1850 VPW (GM Class 2) through the OBD chip. PWM and K-line stay unsupported.

## Bug fixes since alfa-06
- Fixed a full-scale reading dropped by its own range (a full tank, 41 2F FF, decoded to 100.000001646 against a maximum of 100): standard PID rows clamp to the table's range as 4.x did; a custom row's bounds stay a filter (#1004).
- Fixed a custom row's init leaking into the standard rows (a chain that moves the chip to a gateway behind extended addressing froze every Mode 01 value that followed): the baseline is put back between row types (#964).
- Fixed J2534 testers timing out on every ISO15765 request (the device expected the bare payload), and the J2534 server unsubscribing another component's CAN queue before its first CONNECT.

## New features in v6
- New UI design, Quick Setup, improved AutoPID, CAN logger (raw CAN, SQLite, binary, JSON), Dashboard and CAN monitor
- Tailscale VPN; VPN split tunnel (up to 8 routes, .conf import, replaces the Allowed IP and Mask fields, #975); Pause VPN per WiFi network (#976)
- USB devices: ESPNetLink, GPS, USB to Ethernet adapter. ESPNetLink made easy: USB data mode (RNDIS) by default, the header shows the dongle and its LTE/GPS state, a dedicated page, SIM PIN entry, a hint when the GPS gets no fix, a Quick Setup path with no home WiFi; a console-only dongle is detected, and the USB port power is cycled once when a dongle never enumerates
- DTC trouble codes, DBC signals, Rules and Events, Scripting, UDS Tools, J2534, File Manager (SD card and internal flash), System logs and System Monitor pages
- AutoPID and Quick Setup support ISO 27145 (WWH-OBD) vehicles, such as Euro VI Sprinters over 3.5 t that answer only 22F4xx on 29-bit CAN (#298, reported by @billybigpotatoes, protocol findings by @CzarofAK)
- Full configuration Backup and Restore (settings, vehicle profiles and PIDs, code databases, DBC files, scripts, dashboard layout), optional removal of sensitive data, optional encryption, copies on the internal flash and SD card with a checksum per part (#979)
- Logger clock history: every clock step journaled on the SD card, queued records re-based, the clock context on the History chart and the file list (#984)
- ABRP: paced deliveries (5 s minimum, backoff on HTTP 429), a coverage view of the ABRP fields your vehicle provides, ABRP's own error text
- Connections: a socket server that no connection uses is flagged with a one-click CAN bus row. About 10 KB of internal RAM freed (long-lived state in PSRAM)

## Known issues
- A few published vehicle profiles carry rows that never read right on any firmware version (an i3 and Mini state of charge, the bZ4X cell list, a Volvo XC40 state of charge, the pack voltage ranges of the 800 V Hyundai, Kia, Genesis and Xpeng cars). They are being corrected on the profile side.
- A TP 2.0 (VW) reflash over J2534 runs at one round trip per block acknowledgement (about 3 ms over USB, 28 ms over WiFi): flash over the USB cable.
- The ESPNetLink cannot update itself yet; it updates through a WiCAN PRO. A dongle on firmware 1.20 or older must be flashed once over USB.
- J2534: J1850 PWM and K-line are not supported; J1850 VPW is request and response only, no 4x speed.
- A firmware upload started within ten minutes of an update may show that update as "not yet verified" on the Updates card until the ten-minute mark.
