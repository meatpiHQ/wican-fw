# WiCAN PRO 6.0.0-beta.6

Supported device: [WiCAN PRO](https://www.crowdsupply.com/meatpi-electronics/wican-pro) only. Home Assistant: v6 requires the [meatpi-multi-device](https://github.com/meatpiHQ/ha-wican/tree/meatpi-multi-device) branch of the ha-wican integration.

## Before you update

<!-- /release.py:layout -->
- Coming from a v6 alfa or beta: settings, profiles and files are kept; the standard PID rows are re-numbered at the first start. A custom PID you wrote on a v6 alfa or beta against a single-frame reply reads one byte too far: run Test a PID (B0 is the first byte after the CAN id) and move each B index up by one, or re-import the published profile.
- A beta.1 device that parked itself (LED breathing red) updates from safe mode: hold the button until the LED turns sky blue, then yellow, join the device's own access point (its name starts with `WiCAN_`, password `@meatpi#`), open `http://192.168.0.10` and upload the firmware there. Settings are kept.
- Coming from firmware 4.x: the stored files, logs and the saved configuration are erased by the new partition layout; download a backup first (System > Maintenance > Configuration backup). The device restarts once more on its first start.
- In case of a boot loop, perform a [factory reset](https://meatpihq.github.io/wican-fw/factory-reset/factory-reset).

## Bug fixes since beta.5
- Fixed the OBD chip's firmware being downgraded: v6 shipped the chip firmware V2.3.22 and rewrote any chip that was not on it, so a WiCAN PRO that firmware 4.52 had taken to V2.3.24 got a 3557-record downgrade on its first v6 boot, and a chip left in its download mode by an interrupted write could not be recovered from the device. The packaged chip firmware is now V2.3.24 and, as in 4.52, a chip on V2.3.22 or V2.3.24 is left alone. A chip that does not answer in the normal way is now probed at both UART speeds, what it sends is logged, and the recovery download is attempted as 4.x did; only a silent chip is left untouched. The sleep entry waits for a running chip update instead of pulling the chip's sleep pin under a half-written image. New for diagnosis and recovery, on the console and the API only: `obd_chip` (state and version), `obd_chip update --force` / `POST /api/obd_chip/update` (a forced rewrite, about 20 seconds), `obd_chip probe` (what the chip answers at each speed, `probe scan` at every standard speed, `probe auto` lets the UART hardware measure the speed the chip talks at; any other word on the line replaces ATI as what the tools send, so a chip without an application can be asked for its bootloader) and `obd_chip listen` (what the chip sends on its own). Issue #1023.
- New on System > Maintenance: an **OBD chip** card (the chip's state and firmware, a warning when the chip is left on V2.3.22 while the device carries V2.3.24 (the start-up update accepts both, the rewrite below brings it up to date), what the bring-up received when the chip did not come up, a "Read the chip configuration" block with the chip's identity, voltage, protocol, programmable parameters and sleep settings to paste into a bug report, the wire tools, and "Rewrite the chip firmware" behind a confirmation) and a **CAN self-test** card: the OBD chip asks for engine speed the way every OBD app does and the WiCAN's own CAN controller answers it as an engine ECU over the shared bus, so one exchange proves both transceivers, both directions and the wiring, with or without a vehicle. Console twins `obd_chip report` and `can_selftest run`; routes `/api/obd_chip/report`, `/api/obd_chip/probe`, `/api/can/selftest`.
- Fixed the ESPNetLink's APN, username, password and SIM PIN set on the WiCAN (Settings > USB > ESPNetLink, or the Quick Setup's USB step) never reaching the dongle in the default USB Ethernet mode: the dongle kept its own APN (`internet`), a SIM that needs another one (a Simbase SIM needs `simbase`) never attached to the network, and the ESPNetLink page still said the APN was on the dongle. The settings now go to the dongle together with its USB sharing settings, applied by the one restart of the dongle that the pairing already makes; a dongle that already holds them is left alone. WiFi modem mode was not affected.
