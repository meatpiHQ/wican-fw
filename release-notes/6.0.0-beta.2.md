# WiCAN PRO 6.0.0-beta.2

## Before you update
<!-- release.py:layout -->
<!-- /release.py:layout -->

A beta.1 device that parked itself (LED breathing red) takes this update from safe mode: hold the button until the LED turns sky blue, keep holding until it is yellow, join `WiCAN_<device id>` (password `@meatpi#`), open `http://192.168.0.10` and upload the firmware there. Your settings are kept.

## New features
- None.

## Bug fixes
- Fixed a crash on every Home Assistant webhook push with beta.1: a device with the Home Assistant integration registered crashed about 15 s after each start, three times, then parked with the LED breathing red. The firmware update status read the flash from the webhook task; it reads a record taken at boot now.
- A firmware update no longer freezes the device for about 5 s at its start: the whole image area was erased in one go before the first byte arrived, long enough for the task watchdog to log an error. The flash is erased as the image arrives now.
- An OBD app over the WebSocket channel could connect at the wrong moment and receive nothing (the device had just dropped it as a dead client while its handshake was still in flight, and let a third client in). The handshake moment is recognised now.

## Known issues
- A firmware upload started within ten minutes of an update may show the update as "not yet verified" on the Updates card until that ten-minute mark, although it was confirmed.
