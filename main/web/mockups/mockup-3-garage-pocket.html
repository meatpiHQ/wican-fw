Why a pocket layout?

The OpenPort lives in a glovebox. The phone you arm it with lives in your hand. This direction treats the WiCAN AP page as a native-feeling in-car app:

  • One giant ●/■ record button — armable with a thumb, eyes mostly on the road.
  • Five-item bottom bar — Record · Logger · Files · WiFi · System — the same essentials kept across all three mockups.
  • CAN Monitor, Terminal & VPN are gone from navigation entirely.
  • Scales up cleanly: on a tablet the single column becomes the left rail of the desktop "Field Console".

All data shown is placeholder. No backend calls — pure static mock.
